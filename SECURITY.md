# Security policy

This policy applies to every repository in the Foster Rx organisation that does not carry its own.

## Reporting a security issue

Write to **security@fosterrx.com**. Please do not open a public issue or pull request for a security problem.

## What to include

- What you found, and where: the repository, site or address.
- The steps to reproduce it.
- What someone could do with it.
- The version, commit or date you tested against.
- How to reach you, if you would like a reply at a different address.

## What happens next

We acknowledge reports. We may write back with questions while we look into what you sent.

The full policy, including what is in scope and what we ask of reporters, is at <https://fosterrx.com/legal/responsible-disclosure>.
